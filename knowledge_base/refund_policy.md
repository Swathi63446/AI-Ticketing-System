# Refund Policy

## Eligibility
Refunds are available within 14 days of purchase for first-time subscribers on any paid plan. After 14 days, purchases are considered final except in cases of duplicate billing or proven service outage.

## Non-Refundable Situations
- Partial month usage after cancellation
- Add-on purchases (extra storage, premium support packs) once activated
- Accounts suspended for Terms of Service violations

## How to Request a Refund
Customers can request a refund by submitting a support ticket with their order ID and reason for the request. Our billing team reviews requests within 3–5 business days.

## Refund Processing Time
Approved refunds are credited back to the original payment method within 5–10 business days, depending on the bank or card issuer.

## Subscription Downgrades vs Refunds
Downgrading a plan does not automatically trigger a refund for the current billing period. Customers who wish to downgrade and receive a prorated refund must explicitly request it within the eligibility window.

## Chargebacks
Customers are asked to contact support before filing a chargeback with their bank, as chargebacks can result in immediate account suspension while the case is investigated.
