# Premium Plan

## Overview
The Premium Plan is our top-tier subscription, designed for power users and teams that need maximum performance, storage, and priority support.

## Features Included
- Unlimited projects and unlimited storage
- Priority 24/7 customer support with under-1-hour response time
- Advanced analytics dashboard and custom reports
- API access with a 100,000 requests/day rate limit
- Team collaboration for up to 25 members
- Single Sign-On (SSO) and advanced security controls

## Pricing
The Premium Plan is billed at $49/month or $470/year (approximately 2 months free on the annual plan).

## Support Priority
Premium customers' tickets are automatically flagged as high priority in our support queue and are routed to senior support engineers.

## Upgrading to Premium
Existing Basic or Pro customers can upgrade to Premium anytime from the Billing section. The upgrade is prorated and takes effect immediately.

## Cancelling Premium
Premium subscribers can downgrade or cancel anytime; downgrades take effect at the end of the current billing cycle, and any unused API quota does not roll over.
