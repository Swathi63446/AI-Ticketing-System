# Subscription Policy

## Available Plans
We offer four subscription tiers: Free, Basic, Pro, and Premium. Each tier unlocks additional features, storage, and API rate limits.

## Upgrading a Subscription
Customers can upgrade at any time from the Billing section. Upgrades take effect immediately, and the price difference is charged on a prorated basis for the remainder of the current billing cycle.

## Downgrading a Subscription
Downgrades take effect at the end of the current billing cycle, not immediately. This ensures customers keep access to features they've already paid for. Data or features exceeding the lower tier's limits (e.g. extra storage) may be restricted after downgrade.

## Cancelling a Subscription
Subscriptions can be cancelled anytime from Account Settings > Subscription > Cancel. Access continues until the end of the paid period. No further charges will be made after cancellation.

## Trial Periods
New customers on Pro and Premium plans get a 14-day free trial. Customers will be charged automatically when the trial ends unless they cancel before the trial expiration date.

## Plan Changes and Data Retention
If a customer's data exceeds the limits of a downgraded plan, the data is not deleted immediately — it becomes read-only until either the customer upgrades again or removes excess data within 30 days.
