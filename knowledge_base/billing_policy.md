# Billing Policy

## Payment Methods
We accept credit cards, debit cards, PayPal, and bank transfers for all subscription plans. Payments are processed securely and are billed automatically on your renewal date.

## Billing Cycle
Customers are billed either monthly or annually, depending on the plan selected at signup. Invoices are generated automatically and emailed to the account owner within 24 hours of payment.

## Failed Payments
If a payment fails, we will retry the charge up to 3 times over 7 days. Customers will receive an email notification after each failed attempt. If all retries fail, the account will be downgraded to the Free plan and premium features will be disabled until payment is updated.

## Refund Policy
Refunds are issued only for duplicate charges or verified billing errors. Requests must be submitted within 14 days of the charge. Refunds for annual plans, if approved, are prorated based on unused months.

## Invoices and Receipts
Customers can download invoices and receipts anytime from the Billing section of their account dashboard. For tax-related documents (like GST or VAT invoices), customers should update their billing address and tax ID in account settings before the next billing cycle.

## Disputes
If a customer disputes a charge with their bank or card issuer, their account may be temporarily suspended until the dispute is resolved. Customers are encouraged to contact support before initiating a chargeback.
